About us

One company, telling stories across two countries.

Venus Productions is a film, television and live entertainment company working across Sri Lanka and Australia. We develop and produce our own screen productions — feature films and teledramas — from script to screen.

On stage, we organise concerts and stage dramas featuring celebrated Sri Lankan artists, bringing award-winning theatre and much-loved music to audiences overseas.

Whether on screen or in a theatre, our work has one purpose: connecting Sri Lankan stories and talent with audiences at home and around the world.
